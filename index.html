Questions

Is Maani Nexora free?

Yes. It is free to download and use.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it, copy a link, and tap the circle.

Which videos can I save?

Public videos from TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and Pinterest. Private videos cannot be saved.

Where are my videos saved?

In your phone's gallery, and in the Videos list inside the app.

How do I remove sound or save an MP3?

Copy the video link, then hold the circle and choose Remove sound or Extract audio.

Why is it not on Google Play?

It is shared as an APK file on this site. That is why Android asks you to allow the install the first time.

The video did not save. What now?

Check that the link is complete and the video is public, and that you are online. Then tap the circle again.

Privacy

What the app asks for

Maani Nexora has no account and does not ask for your name, phone number or email. It needs internet access to fetch the video you ask for.

Your files

Saved videos and audio stay on your phone. You can delete them from the Videos list at any time.
